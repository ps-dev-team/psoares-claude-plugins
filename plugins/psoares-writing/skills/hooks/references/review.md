# Review

Check a hook for the four mistakes in this order. Each one assumes the ones before it are
fixed. Report each problem with its line, what breaks, and a suggested fix. Ask before
rewriting a hook the user wrote.

## The four mistakes

### 1. Delay

The topic comes too late. If the topic first shows up in line three, lines one and two are
filler: cut them and start with line three. The topic belongs in the first one or two
seconds.

| Delay | Fixed |
| --- | --- |
| Guys, this is one of the craziest things I've ever seen, and when you see it you won't believe it. | Here are three simple ways to improve your gut health. |

### 2. Confusion

The topic is there, but the sentence is hard to follow.

| Confusing | Clear |
| --- | --- |
| These guys built a 30 million dollar empire and the online money they made is hard to earn if you don't develop a journaling practice like they did. | These guys built a 30 million dollar empire, and their secret was a journaling habit. |

Fix: fewer words, simple ones, active voice. Read the hook on its own and ask whether it can
be understood more than one way. If it can, rewrite it until it can't.

### 3. Irrelevance

The topic is clear, but the viewer isn't sure it's for them.

- Say "you", not "I". "If you've struggled with your skin your whole life" beats "I've
  struggled with my skin my whole life": the viewer doesn't have to wonder whether they are
  like you.
- Name a pain they already have. "If you struggle with acne, try these three things" beats
  "Three trends in skincare". Need to have beats nice to have.

### 4. Disinterest

Clear and relevant, but not curious enough. Add contrast: say what they believe and what you
show instead (stated), or say only the alternative when the usual way is obvious (implied).

## Checklist

- [ ] The topic is clear in the first sentence.
- [ ] It can only be read one way.
- [ ] It talks to "you", around a pain or an outcome this viewer has.
- [ ] There is contrast, stated or implied.
- [ ] Subject, stakes, speed, one reading.
- [ ] Sentences in the hook are short.
- [ ] Words, text on screen and visual say the same thing.
- [ ] Text hook: three to five words, two lines at most, natural line break, no punctuation,
      no joining words, about three seconds on screen.
- [ ] The lock-in zone confirms the hook and gives a reason to trust it.
- [ ] Every number, result and claim is real.
- [ ] In long-form, the intro confirms the title and the thumbnail.
