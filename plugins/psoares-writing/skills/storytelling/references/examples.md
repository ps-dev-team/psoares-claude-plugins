# Examples

## Flat and looped: the prince and the dragon

The same story twice. It is the model for rewrite mode: same facts, same characters, different
order and joins.

### Flat

A prince's kingdom is in trouble. He learns there is a valuable resource at the top of a tower,
guarded by a dragon. He rides to the tower, confronts the dragon, takes the resource, comes
home, and the kingdom is saved.

Every beat joins with "and then". Nothing is at risk, there is nothing to guess, and nothing
surprises. It works like a vending machine: you get what you expected.

### Looped

**Stakes.** A plague has been spreading for months, and it has almost reached the town where
the prince's family lives. The only cure is a flower at the top of an ancient tower, deep in
the mountains. But the tower is guarded by a dragon that no knight has ever come back from.
The king begs his son to go. If he doesn't, everyone he loves will be dead by the end of the
week.

*A character with something to lose, a deadline, and a consequence.*

**Big question.** The prince reaches the tower, and the dragon is ten times bigger than he
imagined. He has one sword, no armour and no help. How is he going to get past it?

*The viewer starts guessing: a weak spot, a trick, or he dies.*

**Head fake.** He draws his sword and moves closer. But the dragon isn't attacking. It's
pulling away. It's scared. Then he sees the chain around its neck, bolted to the floor. The
dragon isn't guarding the tower. It's a prisoner. He puts the sword down and breaks the chain,
and the dragon flies off.

*Everyone expected a fight and got a rescue. The clues were there: it pulled away, it was
scared.*

**Re-hook.** He climbs to the top. The flower is in the middle of the room. And next to it
stands the king, his father, the one who sent him.

*One question closes and a new one opens straight away: why is the king there?*

## Hooks

| Opaque | Clear |
| --- | --- |
| You won't believe this. | These are the three garden techniques that doubled my harvest. |
| Something crazy is happening. | Something crazy is happening with Stanley cups. |
| Wait till you see this. | This cup went from a builder's thermos to the most wanted Christmas gift. |

The clear version says what the story is about and still leaves a question open.

## Lenses

Topic: a pop star at the Super Bowl.

- **Common.** What she wore, when she arrived, how she reacted.
- **Less common.** A prediction of what happens next.
- **Rare.** What her presence did to the league's business.

The rare lens makes the story the only one of its kind in the feed.

## "But" turns in a short opening

Something crazy is happening with Stanley cups.
They sell out in seconds and resell for hundreds.
**But** in 2019, Stanley was about to discontinue the cup.
**But** a group of bloggers thought that was a mistake.
**Therefore** they ordered five thousand cups, in pastel colours.
They sold out in five days.
**But** it gets better.

*Each turn opens a question before the last one settles.*

## A villain in teaching content

"Most agencies sell you more ads. We cut your ads in half and make the half that's left work."

*The villain is the usual way of doing it; the hero is the speaker's way.*
