# Voice: examples

A script is one sentence per line, as you would say it:

```
[warm]
In this video, I'll show you how to plan a launch with boards.

[short pause]
On the left, you have your boards.

Each board holds its tasks, and the people working on them.

[pause]
Now, let's create a task.
```

- `[warm]`, `[pause]`, `[short pause]` are directions for ElevenLabs v4 and are not spoken.
- Keep pauses few: a pause to change subject, a short pause between items.
- With your own recording, the same script tells `stt_lines.py` where each line starts.
