# B-roll: people from photos (optional, paid)

Makes footage of people with Higgsfield, from ordinary photos: a sharper photo, the person in a
real-life setting, short clips of them talking or listening. From those clips it makes seamless
loops, overlays, or a fake webcam that talks when the person speaks in a recorded video call.

It is the only part of the plugin that costs money: every step is priced first, nothing is spent
past your balance, and every spend is logged. People are always background (a call tile, a
cutaway), never a presenter.
