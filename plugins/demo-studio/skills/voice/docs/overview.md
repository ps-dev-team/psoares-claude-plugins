# Voice: the clock of the video (optional)

The voice-over, and the time of every line and word in it. The `screen` skill uses those times
to click on the word the voice is saying.

Three ways to get it:

1. **Generate it** with ElevenLabs from a script, with your own cloned voice or any voice.
2. **Bring your own:** a recording from your mic, another TTS, a take from a website.
3. **From your own video:** the sound of a talking-head video drives the demo.

For 2 and 3, the timings come from speech-to-text: a local Whisper (free) or ElevenLabs Scribe.
There is also a tool that shortens long pauses with crossfades, so the voice moves naturally.

No voice at all is fine too: the demo is then timed in seconds.
