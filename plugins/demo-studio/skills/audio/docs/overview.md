# Audio: how it sounds, in numbers

A voice-over can be too quiet, too rushed, or full of long silences, and it is hard to judge by
ear alone, especially next to other videos. This skill measures it and fixes it.

- **Analyze:** loudness, peaks, range, pauses and speech rate for any audio or video file.
- **Normalize:** bring a finished video to a standard loudness in seconds, picture untouched.
- **Pauses:** shorten long silences with crossfades, so a voice-over moves without audible cuts.

Every full render made with the assemble skill is normalized automatically.
